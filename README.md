## Published MAGI-2 examples

The publishers identify these examples as runs of the flagship MAGI-2 and show their submitted prompts. Open each source for the original prompt, output and any shared inputs; media stays with its owner.

Each case has its own card. Linked sources retain the original prompt and footage. A copy-friendly adaptation is a separate untested prompt, not the input used in the source video.


### 1. Cinematic & long takes

#### 1.1. 30-second ramen stall one-take

- **Mode:** Text · 30 s
- **What to look for:** A long camera move with ordered dialogue, service actions, and a passing train.
- **Original prompt / result:** [CreateVision](https://createvision.ai/magi-2#:~:text=One%20continuous%2030-second%20take%2C%20no%20cuts.)

<details>
<summary>Copy-friendly adaptation (untested; not used for the linked output)</summary>

```text
One unbroken overhead-to-eye-level move in a quiet station café shortly before closing. Start on coffee dripping into a ceramic mug, then slide along the counter as a passenger sets down a folded train ticket. The attendant says, “You still have time.” Pull back as a departing train passes the window. Sound: coffee machine, one line of dialogue, swish of the train. No music or subtitles.
```

</details>

#### 1.2. Courtyard across three seasons

- **Mode:** First + last frame · 5 s
- **What to look for:** Locked camera and a seasonal change across two supplied frames.
- **Original prompt / result:** [CreateVision](https://createvision.ai/magi-2#pin-the-first-and-last-frame)

<details>
<summary>Copy-friendly adaptation (untested; not used for the linked output)</summary>

```text
Use the supplied winter and summer images as the exact first and last frames. Lock the camera and preserve the roofline and tree placement. Snow sags off the branches; buds and pale flowers emerge, then give way to green leaves. Keep the seasonal transition smooth with no jump cuts. Sound: wind giving way to birds.
```

</details>

### 2. Dialogue & short drama

#### 2.1. Paris portrait brought to life

- **Mode:** First frame · 8 s
- **What to look for:** Animate one photo into a short exchange with two distinct speakers.
- **Original prompt / result:** [CreateVision](https://createvision.ai/magi-2#dialogue-with-lip-sync-in-english-or-mandarin)

<details>
<summary>Copy-friendly adaptation (untested; not used for the linked output)</summary>

```text
Use the uploaded portrait as the first frame. Preserve both faces, coats, and river-view background. The person on the left says, “Is this our stop?” The person on the right smiles and says, “One more bridge.” Only the active speaker’s lips move. Keep the camera nearly still. Sound: close dialogue and river ambience.
```

</details>

#### 2.2. Mandarin street-food vlog

- **Mode:** Text · 8 s
- **What to look for:** An on-camera seller speaks Mandarin above a busy street sound bed.
- **Original prompt / result:** [CreateVision](https://createvision.ai/magi-2#:~:text=Vertical%20street-food%20video)

<details>
<summary>Copy-friendly adaptation (untested; not used for the linked output)</summary>

```text
Vertical handheld market profile. A vendor places a fresh pastry in a paper sleeve, looks up, and says in Mandarin, “刚出炉，小心烫。” Hold on the vendor’s expression for the final second. Sound: close voice, light street chatter, and the crinkle of the sleeve. No narration or captions.
```

</details>

#### 2.3. Last bowl of the night

- **Mode:** Text · 5 s
- **What to look for:** Two timed shots, a single spoken line, and no music.
- **Original prompt / result:** [Seadanse](https://seadanse.com/blog/how-to-use-magi-2#:~:text=Last%20bowl%20of%20the%20night)

<details>
<summary>Copy-friendly adaptation (untested; not used for the linked output)</summary>

```text
0–2s: Wide shot of a corner bakery at closing time, water running down the front window. 2–5s: Hard cut to the baker sliding the final loaf across the counter, saying, “This one's for you.” Sound: rain outside, paper on wood, and clear speech. No music.
```

</details>

### 3. Advertising & product shots

#### 3.1. Skincare presenter and label

- **Mode:** Image reference · 10 s
- **What to look for:** A product photo anchors the hands-on demo and the closing label shot.
- **Original prompt / result:** [CreateVision](https://createvision.ai/magi-2#product-shots-that-keep-the-label)

<details>
<summary>Copy-friendly adaptation (untested; not used for the linked output)</summary>

```text
Use the product photo to lock the exact container shape and label. In a vertical frame, a presenter lifts the product beside the face and says, “Here's how I use it.” Cut to the bottle on a pale counter at the end, front label facing the lens. Keep all printed words and colors as in the photo. Sound: voice and a small tap on the counter.
```

</details>

#### 3.2. Transfer a camera arc to a packshot

- **Mode:** Image + video reference · 6 s
- **What to look for:** One reference controls the bottle, another the camera path.
- **Original prompt / result:** [CreateVision](https://createvision.ai/magi-2#borrow-the-camera-move-from-another-clip)

<details>
<summary>Copy-friendly adaptation (untested; not used for the linked output)</summary>

```text
Use Image 1 for the appearance of the product and Video 1 only for the camera trajectory. Stage the item alone on a slate plinth. Recreate the slow arc followed by a shallow push-in; do not borrow the reference video’s location or soundtrack. End on a steady readable product close-up. Sound: soft room tone.
```

</details>

#### 3.3. Earbud case macro reveal

- **Mode:** Text · 5 s · 16:9
- **What to look for:** Test engraved lettering, a slow orbit, and a distinct opening sound.
- **Original prompt / result:** [A.I. Creator U.](https://aicreatoru.com/blog/how-to-use-magi-2#magi-2-prompts-you-can-copy)

<details>
<summary>Copy-friendly adaptation (untested; not used for the linked output)</summary>

```text
Macro product setup with a matte travel tin on a smooth slate surface. A tiny embossed brand mark faces camera. A slow half-arc reveals the hinge; the lid opens with one soft click and stops at 45 degrees. Keep the mark and body geometry stable. Sound: one close mechanical click and low room tone.
```

</details>

### 4. Social & UGC

#### 4.1. Living-room group dance

- **Mode:** Text · 10 s · 9:16
- **What to look for:** Vertical social video with a single camera move and distinct audio cues.
- **Original prompt / result:** [CircleAIDance](https://circleaidance.com/magi-2-ai-video-generator#:~:text=Five%20friends%2C%20one%20living-room%20dance)

<details>
<summary>Copy-friendly adaptation (untested; not used for the linked output)</summary>

```text
Four friends make up a short dance in a sunlit studio. In vertical handheld framing, track a single half-circle as they take two side steps, clap together once, then break into laughter. Keep the same four people throughout. Sound: original light percussion, the clap on contact, and voices at the end.
```

</details>

### 5. Motion, animation & sound

#### 5.1. BMX motion stress test

- **Mode:** Text · 6 s
- **What to look for:** A short action diagnostic: follow-through, landing and a sound tied to contact.
- **Original prompt / result:** [CreateVision](https://createvision.ai/magi-2#:~:text=A%20BMX%20rider%20at%20a%20concrete%20skatepark)

<details>
<summary>Copy-friendly adaptation (untested; not used for the linked output)</summary>

```text
Follow a BMX rider at low wheel height as the rider rolls up one ramp, briefly leaves the ground, lands, and coasts out of frame. Preserve one consistent direction and trajectory; do not cut. Sound: tires on concrete, one sharp landing, then fading wheel noise.
```

</details>

#### 5.2. Jazz trio timing test

- **Mode:** Text · 8 s
- **What to look for:** A single lateral dolly with multiple acoustic events to compare against the picture.
- **Original prompt / result:** [CreateVision](https://createvision.ai/magi-2#:~:text=A%20jazz%20trio%20in%20a%20small%20dim%20club)

<details>
<summary>Copy-friendly adaptation (untested; not used for the linked output)</summary>

```text
An upright bassist, a pianist, and a brush drummer play a restrained phrase in a small studio. Slide the camera slowly left to right. Give each musician one distinct audible moment, with the drummer's soft strike aligned to contact. Sound: live instruments in a quiet room, no voiceover or backing track.
```

</details>
