### 0.1. A Father's Little Chef

**45.9K X post views** · @AIwithkhan · Higgsfield creator partnership

*An oversized chef hat and a burnt pancake turn a kitchen mishap into a warm family comedy.*

[![Play A Father's Little Chef](https://pbs.twimg.com/amplify_video_thumb/2125522723820724224/img/IUjb3rXQs4XsOrlO.jpg)](https://x.com/AIwithkhan/status/2125522773258952776)

**Copy-ready prompt (adapted from source):**

```text
Create a 30-second 3D animated family comedy in a softly lit Japanese kitchen. Keep the little girl’s short black bob, pink pajamas, and oversized chef hat consistent. She tries to surprise her father with pancakes: jump from tiny hands mixing batter to flour spraying across the counter, then chocolate smeared on her cheek. Make the pancake uneven and slightly burnt. She serves it proudly; her father takes a bite, hides his surprise, and encourages her. Finish with both parents laughing as she poses like a professional chef. Use warm morning light, expressive faces, and audible bowl, whisk, and skillet sounds. Let the little girl proudly address her father in English before he tastes it.
```

*Source: [Creator’s post, video and prompt](https://x.com/AIwithkhan/status/2125522773258952776) · [Post-view snapshot source](https://teapawt.net/AIwithkhan)*

### 0.2. Tiny Delivery Rider, Giant City

**37.6K X post views** · @AIwithSynthia · Higgsfield creator partnership

*Hand-drawn animation meets real-world scale in a playful New York delivery run.*

**Copy-ready prompt (adapted from source):**

```text
30 seconds, 16:9. Composite a miniature hand-drawn courier with black hair, pink hoodie and red delivery bag into a photoreal New York street. Shoot from her scale: a crosswalk gust pushes her sideways, a giant shoe and a newsstand become climbing and sliding obstacles, and a runaway pickle rolls past the hot-dog stand. Float her across a puddle on a coffee lid. Let a gust lift her by her open delivery bag, then drop her onto a pizza slice. A real hand unexpectedly lifts the slice with her still hanging on. Maintain the same inked silhouette and city lighting throughout. Favor ankle-height macro views and exaggerated but readable motion. Use traffic, water splashes, wind and comic impacts without dialogue.
```

**32.7K X post views** · @AIwithSynthia · Higgsfield creator partnership

**Copy-ready prompt (adapted from source):**

```text
30 seconds, 16:9, semi-realistic anime. Use the supplied character image to lock the skater’s face, hair and proportions. Sweep across Brooklyn Bridge and cut to the industrial tracks: rail grind, a passing subway, and a matched leap down into a parking lot. End low with the pair speeding toward the skyline. Use quick pans, crisp cuts and occasional aerial tracking, but preserve direction and the cat’s real scale. Use wheel rumble, rail grind, taxis, city voices and wind; no music.
```

### 0.4. A Reunion Across Two Worlds

**28.2K X post views** · @AIwithkhan · Higgsfield creator partnership

*One match cut carries the same hand position from a ruined mansion to a bright garden.*

[![Play A Reunion Across Two Worlds](https://pbs.twimg.com/amplify_video_thumb/2125640216140775424/img/qacWXGG-a6NKwrvS.jpg)](https://x.com/AIwithkhan/status/2125640284830835088)

**Copy-ready prompt (adapted from source):**

```text
30-second photoreal short told in eight shots. In a ruined manor, a tearful woman recognizes the injured man she was about to shoot and lowers her bow. Show him by a broken pillar from over her shoulder, then let her kneel, touch his cheek and hold him. Cut in to their joined hands on dusty marble. After a tear hits their fingers, hard cut on that exact framing and hand position: in warm sunlight, they are clean and uninjured in a garden. Follow the same couple along a flowering path; close in on their exchanged look, then arc gently around their kiss and hold on the embrace. Faces and proportions match across the cut. Shoot natural performances with soft grain; use breaths, footsteps, fabric and garden birds, no score.
```

## 1. Cinematic Film Styles

### 1.1. Rainy café, two voices

*Two clean cuts, locked character positions, and Mandarin lines timed by speaker.*

**Copy-ready prompt (adapted from source):**

```text
20 s, 16:9. Set a quiet two-person scene by a rain-streaked café window after dark. Keep the man in charcoal on screen left and the woman in a pale trench coat on screen right. Between them: a dark wood table, two white cups, and a folded boarding pass. Light from one warm pendant above, with cool street reflections behind.
0–7 s: a lightly handheld 35 mm side two-shot. He inches the boarding pass toward her; she keeps her eyes down and traces the cup rim.
7–14 s: hard cut to her over-the-shoulder portrait. She lifts her eyes after a beat and tells him in Mandarin that he always leaves the decision to her.
14–20 s: cut back across the same eyeline to his close-up. Push in almost imperceptibly as he admits in Mandarin that he was afraid to ask her to stay. Hold on the silence.
Sync lips to each speaker. Keep identities, clothes, hand positions and props continuous. Use rain, a faint refrigerator hum and ceramic contact; no other dialogue, music, titles or subtitles.
```

### 1.2. 30-second ramen stall one-take

*A long camera move with ordered dialogue, service actions, and a passing train.*

**Mode:** Text · 30 s

**Copy-ready prompt (adapted from source):**

```text
30 s, one continuous shot. Rainy night at a ramen stall beneath a railway bridge. Begin close to a steaming stockpot as a middle-aged cook in a white towel lifts a bundle of noodles. Drift down the counter to a diner in a yellow raincoat. She orders soy-sauce ramen with an extra egg; he cheerfully offers a generous bowl. Stay with the cooking as he adds broth, pork and egg and slides the bowl to her. Let her laugh into the steam. A train rolls above; paper lanterns sway. Pull away slowly until the whole stall glows in the wet street. Layer rain on canvas, simmering stock, overhead train noise and quiet radio jazz. No cuts.
```

### 1.4. City orbit in one take

**Copy-ready prompt (adapted from source):**

```text
A natural-light, phone-camera take of a woman in a coat walking through a city. Start tracking her full figure from the side; ease toward her front, speed into an arc around her back and rise above her. Follow a descending curve toward her face and hold close briefly, then retreat and lift into a high city-wide view. Keep her pace steady; let the camera drive the tempo. Use natural wide-angle perspective without distorting her face, and preserve stronger parallax in the near background than in distant buildings. One take; hide any unavoidable stitch behind a passing foreground object. Keep the music in step with the move.
```

### 1.5. Paper plane across connected spaces

**Copy-ready prompt (adapted from source):**

```text
Follow one white paper plane through a southern school building at sunset in one spatially continuous shot. It glides off the roof into an open corridor, crosses a classroom past desks and a blackboard and exits a wide open window. Swing from above its wings to beneath them as it descends along the exterior. Follow it through the ground-floor lobby, roll the camera once and level out on exit. Skim the grass and then rise as it slows into a girl's open palm. Maintain the same paper plane, physically plausible gliding, and strong near-to-far parallax. Use an actual opening for each passage; never let it go through a wall.
```

*A staged romance scene with character references, a Korean line, and a timed music entrance.*

**Copy-ready prompt (adapted from source):**

```text

0–2.6 s: from his shoulder, she looks up, breaks into a big smile and says “사랑해요” in Korean at 0.7–2.2 s. Match the four syllables to natural lip movement.
2.6–5.6 s: reverse close-up, he holds his breath for a beat, startled. An instrumental electric-guitar arpeggio enters on the cut.
5.6–8.6 s: return to her smile in the sunlight. 8.6–11.6 s: tighter on him, his expression softens without tears. 11.6–15 s: locked wide shot, both still in place as distant pedestrians move on the steps. Film grain, real skin, no subtitles, logos, extra speech or swapped eyelines.
```
